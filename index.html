Hearth

A private home app for one family.

Hearth helps the members of a single household keep their days in order: their mail, their calendar, their files and their lists. It is not offered to the public, and there is no way to sign up.

What Hearth does

When a member of the household connects their Google Account and asks for help, Hearth can:

  • find and summarise email, prepare replies, and send a message the member asks it to send;
  • read the family calendar and add events such as games, school dates and trips;
  • find, read and create documents, spreadsheets and other files in Google Drive;
  • look up contacts and keep task lists up to date;
  • look up YouTube videos when asked.

Hearth works only with the Google Accounts that members of the household connect themselves, and only for that household.

Your privacy

Hearth never sells information, never shows ads, and never shares information with data brokers. The privacy policy explains what Hearth reads, how it uses it, and how to disconnect it at any time. Use of Hearth is covered by its terms of use.
