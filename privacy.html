Hearth privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Hearth is a private application used by the members of one household. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what Hearth reads from a member's Google Account, how it uses that information, where it is kept, and how to disconnect Hearth at any time. It applies to Hearth and to this site, https://mrdvander.github.io.

What Hearth can access

Hearth reads Google data only after a member of the household connects their own Google Account and approves the access on Google's screen. Depending on what the member approves, Hearth can access:

  • Gmail: read, search and organise messages and labels; prepare drafts; send a message the member asks it to send; move a message to the trash or delete it only when the member asks.
  • Google Calendar: read calendars and events; add or change an event when the member asks.
  • Google Drive, Docs and Sheets: find and read files, documents and spreadsheets; create or update them when the member asks.
  • Google Contacts: read contacts; add or update a contact when the member asks.
  • Google Tasks: read task lists; add, complete or change a task when the member asks.
  • YouTube: read only, to search for videos and see video details.

How Hearth uses it

Hearth uses this information only to do what the members of the household ask of it: for example, to find a message, summarise the day's mail, put a game on the calendar, plan a family trip or keep a list current. It does not use Google data for advertising, or for any purpose other than helping the household.

Automated processing

To produce what a member asks for, Hearth processes information with automated software. Some of it runs on the household's own computers. Some is run for the household by service providers, including automated language-processing services that read a message or file only to return the answer, summary or draft the member asked for. These services receive only what a task needs. Hearth uses them only under terms and settings that do not allow the information to be sold, used for advertising, or used to train or improve their models.

Who can see it

Only members of the household see what Hearth finds, and the owner of each Google Account decides which members may see information from that account. No one outside the household reads it, except the people a member chooses to send something to, a service provider looking into a security problem or abuse, or where the law requires it.

Sharing

Hearth never sells Google user data, never uses it for advertising, never gives it to data brokers or information resellers, and never uses it to decide anyone's credit or lending. It shares information only with the service providers described above, to provide the features a member asks for; with the people a member asks Hearth to send something to; to protect the security of the household's accounts; or when the law requires it.

Where it is kept and how it is protected

Information Hearth keeps for the household is stored on the household's own computers and storage devices, and in private, access-controlled backups, some of them held with service providers. The keys that let Hearth reach a Google Account are kept only on the household's own equipment, readable only by the account that runs Hearth, and are never placed on this site or anywhere public. Connections to Google and to service providers are encrypted.

Keeping, deleting and disconnecting

Hearth keeps information only as long as the household needs it. A member can ask for anything Hearth saved from their account to be deleted; it is then removed from the household's working files, and from backups as far as the backup system allows.

A member can disconnect Hearth at any time: open myaccount.google.com/connections, choose Hearth, and remove its access. Hearth can then no longer reach that account.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Hearth's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Hearth does not use, and does not allow its service providers to use, Google user data, including data from Google Workspace APIs, to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Children

Hearth connects a Google Account that belongs to a member under 18 only with a parent's permission.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
